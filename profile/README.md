### What is Colbe?

Colbe is a guild-based community platform for chatting, organizing, and building with the people you care about. Create communities, manage roles, and communicate the way your community actually works.

### Can I be a part of this?

Yes. We are always looking for developers. If you are experienced with TypeScript and Next.js, reach out at [colbe.cc](https://colbe.cc).
