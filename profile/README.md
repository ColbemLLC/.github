### What is Colbe?

Colbe is a guild-based community platform for chatting, organizing, and building with the people you care about. Create servers, manage roles, and communicate the way your community actually works.

### Can I be a part of this?

Yes! We're always looking for developers. If you're experienced with TypeScript, Next.js, Electron, or Rust, reach out at [colbe.cc](https://colbe.cc).
