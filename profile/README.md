### What is Colbe?

Colbe is a guild-based community platform for chatting, organizing, and building with the people you care about. Create servers, manage roles, and communicate the way your community actually works — no unnecessary clutter, no forced features.

### Can I be a part of this?

We're always looking for developers to join the team! If you're experienced with TypeScript, Next.js, Electron, or Rust — reach out.
